Features

Everything a serious
reader needs. Nothing they don't.

01

Live Text Capture

Point your camera at any page. iOS Live Text selects the words. Your note is created in seconds — no typing required.

02

Book Library

Organise books across Reading, Up Next, Wishlist, and Finished. Scan a barcode or search to add any title instantly.

03

Reading Stats

A heatmap of your reading activity, note type breakdown, favourite authors, and a tag cloud built from your own annotations.

04

Share as Card

Turn any note into a beautiful image card — paper or editorial style, portrait or square — and share it anywhere.

05

AI Book Summary

On supported devices, Marginalis generates an intelligent summary of your notes for any book — powered entirely on-device by Apple Intelligence. Your words never leave your phone.

Not every thought about a book is the same. Marginalis gives each kind of note its own voice — quote, idea, question, reaction, or summary.
